Fides Incorrupta

Fides incorrupta in tenebris perstat. Nullus timor. Nulla dubitatio. Fides nostra est scutum nostrum. Carne cadente, spiritus manet. In nomine Altissimi.

Uncorrupted faith stands firm in darkness. No fear. No doubt. Our faith is our shield. Though the flesh may fall, the spirit remains. In the name of the Most High.
